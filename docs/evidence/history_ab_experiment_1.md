## Constraints Event Intelligence passed to the Events agent

- Before signing, compare venue food-and-beverage minimums, per-head pricing, and vendor guarantees in writing, including how minimums interact with guest-count changes.
- Use the proposed two-week guest-count lock as a planning requirement to confirm with vendors, and establish a seating-change process before the final deadline.
- Create a distinct volunteer-food budget rather than assuming attendee catering covers volunteers.
- Have a workable table-payment process ready before the gala rather than making post-event pledge follow-up the default.
- Test registration before launch, lock sponsor logos a month before printing, and set auction-item delivery deadlines to reduce rush work.
- Bring the catering-risk and donor-experience positions to the board before approving the meal format; the reflections do not establish agreement to cut food or retain it at any cost.
- Past reflections report overruns; plan at least 22% contingency.
