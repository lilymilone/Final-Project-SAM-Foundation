## Calculations

- **minutes saved:** manual minutes − SAM SPACE minutes (negative when SAM SPACE took longer)
- **pct reduction:** minutes saved ÷ manual minutes × 100, only when manual minutes > 0
- **annual hours saved:** measured minutes saved × assumed tasks per year ÷ 60 (a projection)

## Rubric (scored by people, same rubric for both proposals; 0 = missing … 4 = excellent)

- Completeness of required deliverables: Are all eight deliverables present?
- Factual accuracy: Are venue facts, dates, and figures correct?
- Source verification: Can each venue fact be traced to a checked source?
- Financial calculation accuracy: Do totals, net, and break-even add up?
- Financial feasibility: Does the plan respect the budget and cash limits?
- Integration of historical knowledge: Are lessons from past events used appropriately?
- Actionability: Could the board act on it next week?
- Clarity: Is it easy for a board member to read?
- Alignment with the foundation's mission: Does it reflect kindness, belonging, and the cause?
