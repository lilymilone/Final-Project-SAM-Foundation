# Answer quality: before and after the "answer first" fix

Two real questions asked in the app, before and after three changes made on 2026-10-26:
1. The final step now answers the actual question first, in plain English, covering every part of it, with at most three caveats and no internal jargon.
2. Sir's Office uses live research by default.
3. A donations answer lists the gifts that still need a thank-you. Code reads them from the records; the model sees gift codes, amounts, and dates, never donor names.

All text below is quoted from the runs' stored results.
