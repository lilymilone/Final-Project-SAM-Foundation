## 1. Board recommendation

**Prioritize HALI and NYC Health’s Clubhouses; qualify IDHA’s New York availability before advancing**

Pursue exploratory inquiries to Hands Across Long Island (HALI) and NYC Health’s Clubhouses first. HALI’s peer-led mental-health services and concrete community supports provide a practical basis for discussing current needs. Clubhouses offer explicit alignment with dignity, member contribution, mental-health support, and community connection, but require identifying an individual clubhouse partner. Retain the selected Institute for the Development of Human Arts (IDHA) inquiry as a qualification step: its mental-health education aligns with the mission, while New York delivery and substantive belonging activities remain unconfirmed. These are potential leads, not existing relationships or commitments.
- Charitable alignment: HALI combines peer-led mental-health support with community services, including a food pantry and clothing closet. Clubhouses support New Yorkers with serious mental health conditions in pursuing their chosen goals. IDHA provides mental-health education, events, and resources.
- Philosophical alignment: Clubhouses provide the clearest explicit evidence for helping people feel seen and valued through dignity and member contribution. HALI’s peer-led services and community participation activities support human connection. IDHA’s practical contribution to belonging remains unclear.
- Practical feasibility: The drafts ask for contacts, requirements, and current needs rather than proposing a funded activity or volunteer commitment. HALI has established Long Island geography; Clubhouses have established New York City geography. IDHA needs a local-availability check.
- Evidence quality: Mission Discovery reports checked sources for all selected leads. Source verification does not establish partnership availability, program outcomes, accessibility, or collaboration terms.
- Selection limitation: The code-selected shortlist differs from Mission Discovery’s preferred local leads, which included NAMI-NYC rather than IDHA. The selected set does not yet establish three individual New York organizational partners.

- Decide whether a designated person should send the HALI exploratory inquiry and Clubhouses referral inquiry after confirming recipient contacts.

- Choose the human sender and signatory, and review the actual draft wording before sending. Keep all inquiries explicitly exploratory, without funding, volunteer, or scheduling commitments.
- Decide whether to revisit NAMI-NYC if three confirmed New York organizations remain the priority.

**Risks**

- The selected shortlist falls short of confirming three individual New York organizations: Clubhouses is a network, and IDHA’s New York availability is not established.
- Partnership costs, current availability, outside-volunteer requirements, participant interest, and collaboration terms are unknown. No dates are confirmed.
